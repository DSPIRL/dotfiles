When you ask questions or make statements that need attention in a long response, make them easy to spot:
- Put them at the end of the message, under a `## ❓ Questions` or `## ❗️ Statements` heading.
- Prefix each question with an incrementing number `1.`, `2.`, etc, and bold the question/statement itself.
- For blocking decisions in Claude Code, use the AskUserQuestion tool instead.

Example:
```md
## ❓ Questions
1. What's up?
2. How are you?
```

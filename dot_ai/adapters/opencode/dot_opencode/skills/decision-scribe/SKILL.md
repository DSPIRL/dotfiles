# Decision Scribe

## Procedure

1. Capture the decision in one sentence.
2. Record context, serious alternatives, chosen path, and consequences.
3. Use CONTEXT.md for living project philosophy and invariants.
4. Use docs/adr/ for specific architectural decisions.
5. Record reasoning, not activity.

# Decision Scribe Skill

## Procedure

1. Capture the decision in one sentence.
2. Record the context that made the decision necessary.
3. List the serious alternatives considered.
4. Explain why the chosen path fits project values.
5. Record consequences, risks, and what future agents should preserve.

## Artifact Choice

Use `CONTEXT.md` for living project philosophy, domain language, invariants, and conventions.

Use `docs/adr/NNNN-title.md` for a specific architectural decision with tradeoffs.

## Anti-Pattern

Do not log activity. Record reasoning.
